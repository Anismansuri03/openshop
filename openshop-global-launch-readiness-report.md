## 4. Honest cost/obligation disclosures (mission §4C)

OpenShop is free software (MIT), but running a store still costs money: hosting,
a domain, payment-processing fees, email delivery, and the merchant's own tax
compliance responsibilities. Optional integrations (payment gateways, transactional
email, managed Redis) may be paid services. No paid service is a required
dependency of the core platform today. Nothing in this project implies any
affiliation with Shopify or any other trademark owner.
